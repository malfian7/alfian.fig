Overview

Our POS is built for users who have a business or work in the food sector. It brings together detailed pricing for the products customers buy, data processing and management information.

I worked on the app design and on improving its features, so users can reach the information they need quickly.

What's on the screens

  • Shop. A product grid with prices and a running cart.
  • Product detail. Variants, quantity and total price before adding to cart.
  • Sales. Weekly sales stats and a list of orders with totals.
  • Component system. Success states, progress rows, stat cards, order cards and the bottom navigation, built as reusable parts.
